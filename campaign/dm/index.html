Dragon against dragon over the Wildwood - only real players, no AI dragons. Up to 8 dragons, connected directly browser to browser.

  • First to 10 kills wins, or the best after 10 minutes.
  • Eat chickens, goats and sheep to heal.
  • Sit down on the ground, a roof or a rock pillar - and fight from there.
  • Crash into a dragon, a pillar, a wall or a tree and you fall to the ground.
